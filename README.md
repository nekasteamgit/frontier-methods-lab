# Frontier Methods Lab

Open field notes on AI agents, browser automation, lightweight science tooling, and practical benchmarks.

## Positioning

Frontier Methods Lab is presented as a compact public project that documents:
- practical uses of AI agents
- browser-based automation and human-in-the-loop workflows
- lightweight scientific and data workflows
- short benchmark notes and tool comparisons

The framing is intentionally editorial and technical at the same time: it looks like a small public lab notebook rather than a pure marketing site.

## Why this repo exists

The goal is to accumulate credible technical notes, experiment logs, and short explainers that can later support natural references to relevant tools, products, workflows, and case studies.

## Editorial pillars

1. AI agents in real work
2. Browser automation and operator tooling
3. Practical science / data workflows
4. Benchmarks, methods, and field notes

## Suggested content formats

- short method notes
- comparison pages
- experiment logs
- mini case studies
- resource roundups

## Repo structure

- `docs/editorial-map.md` — angles, anti-footprint rules, future link slots
- `content/posts/` — evergreen article drafts or published markdown notes
- `content/notes/` — shorter field notes and benchmark snippets

## First angles to publish

- Why browser agents need slower, more human workflows
- Small AI benchmarks that matter more than leaderboard headlines
- How lightweight science tooling is changing solo research habits
- Practical differences between agent demos and usable operator systems

## Publishing logic

This repo can stay a content-first public project or later become a small static site.
