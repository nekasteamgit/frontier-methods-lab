# Editorial map — Frontier Methods Lab

## Core concept

A faux-project that looks like an open technical notebook focused on AI methods, browser automation, and practical science tooling.

## Why it works for future links

The project can naturally reference:
- software tools
- product case studies
- implementation guides
- benchmark resources
- external examples and further reading

That gives future room for contextual links inside:
- "related tools"
- "further reading"
- "implementation notes"
- "recommended stack"

## Anti-footprint rules

- keep the tone educational and technical
- avoid obvious sales language
- vary article structure and length
- do not overload every note with outbound links
- mix evergreen notes with timely observations

## Initial publication sequence

1. 3 to 5 posts without commercial links
2. 1 resource page with carefully chosen references
3. later, contextual links to target sites where they fit naturally

## Candidate target themes

- AI productivity workflows
- browser automation
- observability for agents
- research tooling
- practical benchmark culture
